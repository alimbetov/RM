### UI-P01 — Morning Brief
Role-appropriate compact view after night completion; show major risks/opportunities only from qualified admitted tickets, separate data-quality notices and explicit partial scan status. Counts over current authorized eligible population before top-K, no claim of complete coverage when scan incomplete. Show title, metric code, comparison, affected scope, asOf, evidence link, optional admitted check. Never invent a business recommendation to fill an empty slot. If fully qualified scan has no findings, show that explicitly; if incomplete, show coverage limitation instead. Reuse existing Radar and report views, no new scheduler.

### UI-P02 — Business Pulse
KPI tiles with metric code, value, unit, Hint, relative/absolute delta and sparkline of a fixed qualified historical window. Sparkline gaps remain gaps; comparison absent marked unavailable. Do not aggregate unlike SKU quantities or currencies. Arrow/color show direction with explicit sign; risk/opportunity only if existing qualified policy classifies it. No composite health score. Excel native sparkline or trusted preconfigured mini-chart, React ECharts, static PDF.

### UI-P03 — Business Heatmap
Rows eligible geography or warehouse, columns eligible product groups, cell value existing qualified relative metric (e.g. YOY_RATIO formatted once as percent). Single metric/comparison/unit per matrix. Stable row/column sort and bounded pagination; explicit unknown/partial/not-applicable cells distinct from qualified zero. Fixed documented symmetric color scale per comparison view with clipping explicitly indicated, not independently autoscaled by row. Use neutral zero and distinguish increase/decrease, not unproven good/bad. No fabricated combinations; hide unauthorized rows/cells and avoid revealing counts. Excel conditional formatting on typed cells; React ECharts Heatmap; PDF fixed printable matrix. City and booked geography restrictions remain.

### UI-P04 — Top Winners & Losers
Rank only comparable qualified rows at the same nonoverlapping scope level, metric, currency/UOM, period and comparison method. Apply existing absolute/relative materiality eligibility first, exclude missing/zero-baseline ratios and incompatible comparisons; separately label qualified zero-to-positive activity if admitted. Sort signed delta or growth using one declared rank basis, tie-break stable scope key. Show absolute change alongside relative change when available; small baseline cannot alone confer high priority. Default caption is leaders of increase/decrease, not business profit winners or losers unless domain policy supports that classification. Top-N configurable within existing bounds, not evidence of scan completeness.

### UI-P05 — Drill-Down
Tenant → Region → City → Warehouse (only supported hierarchy) and Product Group → Subgroup → SKU are independent axes; product filter is not a geographic child. Use current permission checks at each step, historical membership for period, consistent revisions or explicit refresh. Show breadcrumb, selected scope, metric code, period, quality and evidence. Tenant total may represent visible subset, label accordingly. Parent growth never masks a qualified child decline. Do not infer causality from localization. Excel filterable Details/Groups sheets, React/Radar authorized detail link, PDF selected path and bounded detail table. Do not embed hidden unauthorized data.

### UI-P06 — Contribution Analysis
Only qualified additive metric with same currency/UOM/method and a complete mutually exclusive child partition under the same scope/period evidence. Identity check: parentCurrent-parentPrior = sum(childCurrent-childPrior). Include prior-only/current-only eligible children with qualified zeros only; disclose unclassified remainder only when independently reconciled. If reconciliation fails or coverage incomplete, block full waterfall; no fabricated OTHER or forced balancing. EXACT_DISTINCT, ratios and semi-additive metrics excluded absent separately qualified method. Show signed absolute contributions and previous/current totals; not causal attribution. Compute deltas and reconciliation in existing analytical payload, not in renderer. Native preconfigured waterfall or stacked bar/table equivalent as supported; optional PDF only after fidelity qualification.

### UI-P07 — Historical Range Selector
Default 12M view with matching prior-year months where admitted; 3M/6M/12M/24M optional display windows of existing 24M dataset. Range change filters presentation only; no silent method change, no inferred comparator, no hidden requalification. 24M view is historical trend, not promise of 24 additional comparator months. Show actual start/end, displayed coverage and analytical required coverage separately. Missing periods remain explicit slots. React selector, Excel existing filters/named ranges or preconfigured views, PDF records selected range. Extra server fetch if needed only through existing authorized bounded read; no duplicate data bank.

### UI-P08 — What Changed
Compare two successive admitted publication manifests for the same tenant, visibility scope, compatible policy and comparison basis; preserve previous and current actual asOf. Classify NEW (new logical episode), UPDATED (semantic content/evidence change relevant to user), RESOLVED (qualified disproof only), UNCHANGED (same relevant content; render timestamp alone not update), STALE/UNKNOWN (lost qualification), with no double counting per logical key. ACL loss hides item, not resolution; policy/scope mismatch or absent prior snapshot gives comparison unavailable, not fabricated counts. Revisions must be fenced so stale workers cannot overwrite current state. Reuse lifecycle/audit evidence and existing Radar; no new change-tracking engine or separate notification for every 3h refresh.

## UI-P04/P05/P08 final safeguards
FULL_DISTRIBUTION uses the entire authorized metric-applicable expected population, including eligible proven-zero rows, before materiality or top-N. Establish scope and interval completeness independently; missing/partial expected rows or budget exhaustion block complete-distribution claims, not silently shrink the denominator. MATERIAL_FINDINGS evaluates all required applicable scopes and applies admitted materiality only to qualified findings/ranking/ticket selection; it never supplies the denominator of FULL_DISTRIBUTION. Both reuse compatible evidence. These are logical selection purposes, not new engines or public enums.

Business scope identity (tenant plus stable authorized business scope keys, independent geography/product axes) alone anchors existing business PatternTicket/InformationTicket keys. G01–G07, grouping policy, metric, anchor, comparison mode and membership digest identify presentation groups only. Re-ranking or changing layout cannot create, resolve, suppress or re-key a business ticket. A future concentration-change pattern, if independently admitted, is keyed to the real business population and method, never the mutable band. No new pattern is admitted here.

Default HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE drill-down must reproduce the published report's exact authorized scope, metric/method, period/asOf, payload digest, generation/proof/source revision manifest (or owner-source evidence), and grouping policy/membership digest where applicable. Reauthorize every request against current permissions, verify the complete historical result and detail totals or reject; retained exact details or provably reproducible pinned evidence may be used. A revision number/digest alone is not a historical snapshot. If evidence expired, cannot be reconstructed, membership mismatches or permissions no longer cover the original scope, fail closed with localized unavailable/forbidden reason; no substitution of current facts, no silent narrower denominator. Offer explicit LATEST_DATA as a new authorized query/payload with new asOf and revision, clearly not historical drill-down. Do not expose revoked detail or counts; no mandatory new storage or indefinite generation retention. Paginate with pinned manifest, fail on drift. Previously downloaded files cannot be remotely revoked; future server reads/downloads remain subject to existing ACL.
