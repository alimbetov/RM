# RM-LOC-CUR-R1 — Country Profile, Regional Dictionary & Universal Monetary Analytics

Status: owner-agreed direction, 2026-11-28. Implementation baseline base_dev 04ad57cd8a847232f8e64d92dd47741115a058b9. KZ-only admission remains unchanged. No new acceptance family, country activation, statutory accounting claim or production admission.

## 1. Country profile and tenant onboarding
- Kazakhstan is the first supported market, not a global invariant. Country code ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, currency ISO 4217, explicit IANA timezone, supported locale, territory classifier and mapping version, approved FX source and profile version. Country profile is an immutable/versioned defaults reference; tenant policy is the actual authority.
- KZ default and mandatory accounting/public-price currency KZT in current MVP. Other markets disabled until separate legal, operational, FX, geographic, IAM and numerical qualification. Country profile examples: UZ/UZS, KG/KGS, AM/AMD, TJ/TJS, AZ/AZN, BY/BYN, RU/RUB; examples are not approved provider integrations.
- Reuse tenant_currency_policy, tenant_allowed_currency, immutable currency policy events and owner authorization. Initialize once in authorized onboarding, not silently on report read. Profile revision never silently rewrites existing tenant policy. Accounting currency change requires an approved effective-date migration and history comparability policy, not ordinary CRUD. Purchase, internal sales, public price, valuation, pricing anchor remain distinct purposes.
- One accounting currency per tenant/policy version; standard monetary reports derive it from the tenant policy, not from the workbook or client filter. Currency-specific source reads remain diagnostics; not consolidated business totals. R12 can show original currencies/rates separately.

## 2. Geographic extension
- Existing ref_kato_territory and KATO hierarchy remain admitted KZ implementation. Do not reinterpret KATO codes as universal keys. Future reference identity includes country, classifier, national code and historical mapping version; semantic REGION/DISTRICT/LOCALITY mapping independent of numeric depth. Preserve stable historical identities, administrative change evidence and tenant-owned business locations. No cross-country or cross-tenant rollup by code alone.
- Extend via national dictionary import and mapping qualification, preserving existing KZ behavior. Non-KZ does not enter existing KATO-only routes until qualified. No immediate parallel geography engine.

## 3. FX and accounting basis
- Reuse FxRateProvider and immutable rate snapshot contract: provider, base/quote ISO currency, nominal, rate, effective date, revision, payload digest, synthetic flag. Provider-specific format/calendar adapter may be necessary; no assumption that a URL change suffices.
- If rate is quote units per nominal base units, accounting amount = original amount * rate / nominal for direct quote into accounting currency. Require positive nominal/rate, exact decimal intermediate arithmetic, approved recognition date, rounding and valuation method. Same currency uses identity conversion, no external rate. Reciprocal/cross rates require explicit approved method and evidence; no silently inferred or current-rate historical conversion.
- Preserve original money and immutable accounting amount, policy/method version and FX evidence for converted facts; distinguish realized/valuation/price candidate. Smart FX Shield repricing evidence is not automatically accounting recognition evidence. Missing FX, unqualified rates or mixed basis must block consolidated monetary KPI, not silently filter a foreign sale out of the total. No synthetic rates in production.
- L0 owner facts and L1–L3 aggregate the same admitted money basis. Exact distinct count and currency-compatible populations; AOV uses matched denominator. No Excel-side currency conversion or average of ratios. Raw currency-specific L0/L3 reads may stay available under existing authorization as diagnostic observations, not consolidated reports.
